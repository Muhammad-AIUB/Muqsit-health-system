## At a glance

- The ℞ → Drug-history mirror claims only what it INSERTED (`mirrorClaims`), never what it would write; what it owns travels with the VISIT (`rxMirrorOwned`); a resumed draft's lines are re-stamped to today.
- A patient's list is never added to by sending the whole list from one device: lines added are APPENDED BY THE SERVER under the row's lock (`POST /patients/:id/drug-history`); a client sends only what it adds.
- Drug history strings `dd/mm/yyyy: Drug — dose — food — duration` (+ `(note)`, `(cont)`); legacy `Current:`/`Past:` must parse; Current vs Distant past is derived, never stored (+2 more)
- How it works — ℞ → Drug history mirror: every medicine on today's ℞ is stamped with `ptDate` and merged into `drugHistory` live by an effect in `MuqsitContext`.
- ℞ mirror withdraws only what it INSERTED (`rxMirrorOwned`, riding draft, `incompleteRx` and device mirror); a medication typed by hand can never be deleted by editing the ℞ (+3 more)
- A `>>>` taper never leaves its medicine: written only under a named medicine directly above; a tapered medicine keeps its name; unwritable lines are held, red, and Save refuses (+4 more)
- ℞ mirror echoes and completes nothing: a medicine with no dose is recorded with no dose; nothing in the app fills a dose in.
- `rowsFromRxItems` owns the head/taper/note classification; never re-derive it (a taper turns into a second medicine); ℞ notes are skipped by the mirror.
- ℞ mirror never persists by itself; `drugHistory` keeps its existing writers and rides `incompleteRx`; the mirror waits for `drugHistoryHydratedRef` to match the loaded patient.
- ℞ mirror entries are stripped from the printed Drug history (`PrescriptionView#buildHtml`), matched on the exact entry, so a hand-typed same-date medication still prints.
- Drug history modal is NOT editable (both tabs): no input over a STORED entry; do not re-add an editor without asking.
- "+ Add medicine" is ADD-only, never an edit of a stored row; it is the ℞ pad (`showHabits` off); a line it cannot write (`unwritableLines`) stops the Add, nothing added or cleared (+3 more)
- Background patient fetch merges `drugHistory` by BLOCKS (`mergeWithRecord`), never overwrites it with `p.drugHistory`; known limit: a ℞ change after Save & print is undone by a reload.
- Drug history selection: ticks, Select all and Move to Rx (N) append ticked blocks to today's ℞; the only write is into `rxItems`, nothing edits an entry.
- Move to Rx invents nothing: a blank dose, food or duration reaches the pad blank.
- Move to Rx block = head medicine plus its `(cont)` rows, bounded by next medicine, a note or its date group; a taper must never reach the ℞ without the medicine it qualifies.
- Move to Rx skips only a line already on the pad, drug compared by `sameMedicine` (`lib/medicineKey.ts`), never letter for letter; no medicine dedupe between ticked rows (+2 more)
- Drug history notes and nameless rows are not selectable; selection is per tab.
- Drug history selection controls need `rx.medicines`, the ℞ pad's own key; without it the modal renders as before, so no one writes medicines around the pad lock.
- Drug history modal and Records section show the GENERIC, never the trade name — display only; nothing guessed: an unmatched or ambiguous label is shown as stored with a note (+2 more)
- How it works — The Drug history modal cannot correct or remove a stored entry; a start date and duration are put right in Patient's Prescriptions & reports → Drug history → Edit.
- Records Drug history table: End = Start + days of every line; Total = Σ(units a day × days); nothing guessed — unreadable cells give a dash, a total is not rounded (+3 more)
- Drug history table Edit changes only start date and durations; saves the BLOCK via `PATCH /patients/:id/drug-history` by exact text, 409 on conflict; no answer is not "not saved" (+3 more)
- Drug history table Add writes the record through the server's append (`addDrugHistoryEntries(lines, { toRecord: true })`); its duplicate check is only against the same start date.
- Drug history table: a continuing medicine is ONE row until a prescription visit lacks it, then it has stopped (End = day before); Download prints the table as shown (+2 more)
- How it works — Drug history table tests: `drugCourse.test.ts`, `DrugHistoryTable.test.tsx`, `RecordsHistorySections.test.tsx`, `MuqsitContext.drugHistoryEdit.test.tsx`; server `drug-history-edit.spec.ts`.
- Drug history ⊘ Hide takes the WHOLE section off the print and is ON by default (`hideDrugHistory` starts `true`, `resetEditor` sets it back); entries untouched; no per-line ticks.
- The `Lock` in `LeftColumn` stays: an assistant without `rx.drugHistory` must not be handed a patient's medications by the read-only view.

# The rules, in full

## Patterns We Do Not Use

_From the root `CLAUDE.md`._

- We do not let a mirror remember what it WOULD write as what it wrote. The ℞ → Drug-history mirror kept "the entries today's ℞ produces" as its own and withdrew them when a line left the pad — but it inserts a block only when the very same text is not there already, so a line the doctor had typed into Drug history by hand that read exactly like a ℞ line (Move to Rx writes the same text) was claimed, and taking the medicine off the pad deleted the hand-typed record (independent review, 18/10/2026; older than that day's change). It now claims only what it inserted (`mirrorClaims` in `client/src/lib/rxDrugHistory.ts`) — and what it owns is part of the VISIT (`rxMirrorOwned`, in the draft and the device mirror): the first fix kept it in one device's memory, and the second review found that a second, mirrored device then never claimed the lines and could not withdraw a medicine it took off the pad. Ownership that a feature depends on travels with the state it describes. And it is decided by the operation itself, not by a second reading beside it: the claim was worked out before the withdrawal it depended on, and a medicine whose taper was deleted was never claimed again (release review). The same review found the mirror's other blind spot: a draft parked yesterday and resumed today kept its ℞ lines at yesterday's date, and under the new "Current = the previous visit" rule an unprinted visit read as the previous one; the resumed draft now tells the mirror those lines are its own, and they are re-stamped to today.

- We do not add to a patient's list by sending the whole list from one device. "+ Add medicine" after Save & print first wrote the editor's whole Drug history — a copy that follows the ℞ pad: a medicine added to the pad after the print and never printed went onto the record as given today, and a printed medicine the pad no longer held was erased from it. The fix built the list from "the record as last read" — which is one device's copy: another device's print, landing first, was erased by it, and a write queued across a patient switch wrote one patient's list into another's (two reviews, before release). Lines added are APPENDED BY THE SERVER under the row's lock (`POST /patients/:id/drug-history`); a client sends only what it adds. A whole-list write that remains (Save & print's own) is one device's word and waits its turn behind what that device already sent.

## Storage string protocols (the data model hiding in strings)

_From `client/CLAUDE.md`._

- **Drug history (per-patient `Patient.drugHistory`):** `dd/mm/yyyy: Drug — dose — food — duration`, notes `dd/mm/yyyy(note): …`, tapering `dd/mm/yyyy(cont): dose — food — duration`. Legacy `Current:`/`Past:` prefixes must keep parsing. Current-vs-Distant-past is **derived** — never store the bucket. **Since the physician's sheet of 18/10/2026 (item 2) Current is the PREVIOUS visit** (the latest real date before `ptDate`) plus what was added to Current by hand today; today's ℞ mirror is listed in NEITHER tab (exact entries of `rxDrugHistoryEntries(rxItems, ptDate)`, counted); older dates, a future date and the no-date marker `01/01/2000` are Distant past. One function, `lib/drugHistorySplit.ts#splitDrugHistory`, pinned in `drugHistorySplit.test.ts`; the modal blocks Current one date at a time so a taper never crosses visits. A draft from an earlier day, resumed, re-stamps its own ℞ lines to today (the mirror owns them through the draft's `rxMirrorOwned`; a draft from before that record takes its ℞ lines at the old date as owned) — or an unprinted visit would read as "the previous visit". Today's ℞ is hidden a whole BLOCK (medicine + tapers) at a time, and the two dates of Current are headed apart ("Previous visit · …", "Added today · …"). Before this, Current was "dated today". `rxAlerts`' current drug history (90 days) is its own rule, unchanged.
  - **The ℞ pad mirrors into it live (`lib/rxDrugHistory.ts`, physician's decision 2026-09-07).** Every medicine written on today's ℞ is stamped with `ptDate` and merged into `drugHistory` by an effect in `MuqsitContext`, so it appears in **Current medications** as it is typed and reads as Distant past on the next visit. Before this the two lists never spoke and a patient prescribed Napa today showed "0 current" — the earlier `ptDate` fix corrected the split *boundary*, which could never put a medicine into a list nothing was feeding. Five rules hold it, each pinned in `rxDrugHistory.test.ts`:
    - **It withdraws only what it added.** `syncRxDrugHistory(stored, prevDerived, nextDerived)` removes an entry only if the mirror contributed it AND it has left the pad; `rxDerivedRef` (keyed by patient id, so another patient's pad is never consulted) is that memory — and since 18/10/2026 it is EDITOR STATE, `rxMirrorOwned` (it was a ref, `rxDerivedRef`): the blocks the mirror INSERTED (`mirrorClaims`), not what the pad writes — a block already in the list (typed by hand, e.g. then moved to ℞) is never claimed, so it is never withdrawn. Like `rxAdviceOwned` it rides the draft snapshot, `incompleteRx` and the device mirror, and `resetEditor` sets it to `null` ("not known"): a reload and a second, mirrored device know what the mirror owns — kept in one device's memory, a second device never claimed the lines and could not withdraw a medicine it took off the pad (second review). `null` (a draft or a peer from before it existed) claims what the pad writes, as before. What it claims is decided AFTER the blocks that left the pad are withdrawn, by the sync itself (`reconcile` reports what it inserted) — decided before, a medicine whose taper was deleted matched its own old line and was never claimed again, so taken off the pad it stayed in the history for good (release review). It keeps what it owns through Save & print (print, correct, print again leaves only the second print's lines — a first fix that dropped the ownership at Save & print left a removed line in the history for good, caught by the re-review); a draft resumed on a LATER day drops its claim on lines its earlier save put on the record (`withoutBlocksIn`, with the record's `drugHistory` from `loadPatient` / the reload path). **A device that receives a PRINTED visit through the mirror after midnight keeps the visit's date** (`applyMirrorSnapshot`, when the snapshot's `rxCompletedPid` is its `currentPatientId`; release review, 18/10/2026): it used to move the date to today like any stale snapshot, and the mirror then withdrew the printed ℞ line as its own and wrote it back under today's date — a Save & print from that device stored only today's copy, the medicine kept and its real date lost. Not moving the date was chosen over `withoutBlocksIn` there because a snapshot carries no record to check against (the receiving device's copy may predate the print), and because the device that printed the visit keeps its date: moved on one device only, the two would hold one visit a day apart, and the next snapshot back would carry the moved date to the first. An unprinted visit still moves to today and re-stamps its own lines. Not covered: a snapshot from a build without `rxCompletedPid` is taken as unprinted. Pinned in `MuqsitContext.test.tsx` ("a visit mirrored to another device across midnight"). A medication the doctor typed into the modal by hand can never be deleted by editing the ℞. An empty `prevDerived` — a reload, a patient switch — is always safe: it withdraws nothing.
    - **⚕️ A taper never leaves its medicine (2026-09-29).** A `(cont)` entry names no drug; it belongs to the entry directly above it, by position alone. The sync therefore works in BLOCKS (a medicine + its tapers): a changed block is withdrawn whole and its replacement returns to the same place when it is the same medicine (taper edited/added/removed), else to the end as one piece. Appending a lone new taper used to hand it to whichever medicine was last — a dose for the wrong drug. Likewise a taper is deduped only under an identical head: two medicines tapering `0+0+2 — — 7 days` are two instructions. **And a repeat is decided by the WHOLE block (independent review, 18/10/2026; older than that day's release):** two identical blocks are one fact, but the same medicine written again with a block of its own keeps its own line, so its tapers stay under it. Decided line by line, a pad of Napa, Bex, Napa (the identical line) with `>>> 0+0+1` under the second Napa wrote `[Napa, Bex, (cont)0+0+1]` — the taper read as Bex's, and Move to Rx would have put it under Bex. Folding the taper into the first Napa was not chosen: that line has no taper on the pad, and had it one, the two tapers would read as ONE regimen, the first then the second, which no line says. A block is found in a list only WHOLE (`findBlock`: no taper straight after it) — found as the start of a longer block, taking the plain Napa off withdrew the head of Napa + taper and wrote the taper twice. Consequence: Move to Rx with both Napa blocks ticked adds the first and skips the second as `already on ℞`, taper and all (`appendBlocks` compares first lines) — nothing goes under the wrong medicine, and the footer counts it. **⚕️ A taper with no medicine DIRECTLY above it is not written (release review of 551a120, 18/10/2026):** at the top of the pad or straight under a note — including where the medicine whose name was cleared stood there: the pad's own reading (`rxItemsFromRows`) dropped a line whose name was cleared and kept its `>>>` lines until the fix below, and the pad gives a note no `>>>` of its own (a note may be a medicine typed by hand, "Insulin as before"). Written, such a taper went under whatever entry the history held above it — a dose for the wrong medicine — and the merge with the record then wrote that entry twice: record `[Bex]`, pad `[>>> 0+0+1, Napa]`, reload → `[Bex, Bex, (cont), Napa]`. It stays on the pad, marked in red, and the visit cannot be saved or printed with it (below) until the line it belongs to is given a medicine's name. "+ Add medicine" (`unwritableLines`) and Move to Rx (`blocksFromRows`) refuse it the same way. Pinned in `rxDrugHistory.test.ts`, and in `rxDrugHistory.prop.test.ts` over pads built to hold such lines (run once at 30,000 runs). **⚕️ Closed the same day — at the source first (physician: "dangerous bug"; three reviews).** A medicine whose name was cleared while ANOTHER medicine stood above it used to hand its `>>>` lines to that medicine: `rxItemsFromRows` dropped the nameless line and kept its tapers, `savePrescription` filled the medicine before's name into them, and `prescriptionDoc` printed them under it. What holds now: **(1) A medicine with `>>>` lines keeps its name.** Emptied and the box left, the name it had (with its generic) comes back, and the pad says how to take it off (× in ✎ Edit, which takes its `>>>` lines) or change it (type or pick the new name) (`MedicinePad`, `keepNameIfTapered`). It goes back only onto the very `>>>` lines it had when the box was entered — the same row objects: rows are keyed by position, and lines drawn again under the caret (another device, a move) are another medicine's, so then nothing is put back and the line is marked (third review: Barcavir 0.5 mg went back over 1 mg's `>>>` line). Picking a line up to drag it leaves the box first. The notice goes when lines are moved or taken off, and with the visit (the OPD pad is keyed by `editorEpoch`). **(2) A `>>>` line stands only under a medicine line.** × never tidies away the line straight above a `>>>` line (a blank medicine line or a blank note), and a blank line with `>>>` lines has a × of its own that takes them with it (the first fix missed this: × put the next `>>>` line under the medicine above). ▲ / ▼ and a drag never put a block directly above a block that begins with a `>>>` line (`canMove`). A `>>>` line saved with no medicine line above it — at the top, or straight under a note; only data saved before this holds one — is drawn as a line of its own with no name (`rowsFromRxItems`): drawn as a `>>>` line it was the taper of whatever an edit put above it (third review: ▲ on a medicine, × tidying away a blank note). **(3) What cannot be written is not written, and is refused — never put under another medicine.** `rxItemsFromRows` writes a `>>>` line only under a named medicine directly above it (`rxDrugIndexByRow` follows); `writableItems` reads items from elsewhere the same way and counts what it leaves out; `unwritableRows` names the lines the pad cannot write — such a `>>>` line, and a line with a dose, food or duration and no name — which the pad marks in red (a status, not an alert). Save & print, Preview PDF and "Save to complete later" refuse while the pad holds one, each in a box that has to be read (`tell()`; `rxPadUnwritable`, counted from the pad's hold since 19/10/2026 — (4); `savePrescription` also refuses on the items themselves, and its name fill stops at a note); the IPD sheet's two Saves refuse BEFORE touching the follow-up boxes (the first fix moved the typed vitals into the log and then refused); the template editor's Save refuses; a template that begins with a `>>>` line is added without it, and says so; "+ Add medicine" uses the same function. A new visit — `resetEditor`, or another patient arriving through the device mirror (third review) — redraws the pad (`editorEpoch` → `usePadSync`): a pad holding only such lines gave `rxItems` = [] like a reset, and one patient's lines stayed on the next one's pad. **(4) What cannot be written is HELD with the visit (19/10/2026).** Such a line stays out of `rxItems` — every reader of `rxItems` (the printed sheet reads a nameless line as a `>>>` line) stays safe without a check of its own — and the editor holds the pad's ROWS beside it while one is on the pad (`rxPadHeld`, `lib/rxPadHold.ts`; written by `usePadSync`, in the same effect that writes `rxItems`; drawn from a hold — when the pad is first drawn, and at a new visit — `rxItems` are made what the drawn rows write, so the pad and every reader of `rxItems` read the same lines (independent review: the first version drew a `>>>` item appended after the held rows as a line of its own while `rxItems` kept it under the medicine above, and drew it again at a new visit and with each snapshot from an older build). The hold rides the draft snapshot, `incompleteRx` and the device mirror, is read back with `readPadHold` (anything that is not a list of pad rows is `null`, never a throw) and is cleared by `resetEditor`. Every drawing of the pad — the ℞ column mounting (another tab, the phone layout), a new visit, a change made elsewhere, a hold arriving from a draft or a mirrored device — is `drawPad(rxItems, held)`: the held rows come back only while they still fit the `rxItems` they were taken with — exactly those, or those with medicines APPENDED after them, beginning with a medicine or a note (a template, Add Bulk Medicine, Move to Rx). Two things never fit: items that hold a line they cannot write themselves (`writableItems`; only data a build from before 18/10/2026 wrote — compared as the pad writes them, such a line was set aside unseen), and an append that BEGINS with a `>>>` line, which any device on a build without the hold sends, most often a `>>>` line its doctor added under the last medicine (drawn after the held lines as a line of its own, it stayed in `rxItems` under the medicine above, and each snapshot from that device drew it once more: second pass of the independent review). The pad then shows the items as they read — as the printed sheet and every other reader of `rxItems` reads them, the `>>>` line under the medicine before it — and says the held lines went. Anything else, and they are not laid over a prescription they were not written beside: the pad says what went, in a box (`droppedLines` → `tell()` in `RightColumn`): when only `rxItems` changed, what this pad showed; when a hold arrived with a draft or another device's state, only what of THAT hold could not be drawn — the other device's state is its word on its own lines, so a line put right or taken off there is not announced here as lost (the first version did, with "write it again", on the mirrored device: independent review); at a new visit, only the new visit's own lines, never the last one's. A line counts as still there only when the new pad holds it as a line that cannot be written (in either language) — "the same dose at the same place" was once taken for it, and hid a real loss behind a common dose.
