# Domain — Drug history

> Part of the shared vocabulary ([glossary](../DOMAIN.md)). Each entry is a concept with the physician's decisions behind it, moved here word for word from `docs/DOMAIN.md` on 2026-11-15. Keep it current in the same commit as the change (PRIME DIRECTIVE rule 7), and its line in the glossary with it.

## Drug history

- **Drug history is read by its generic names (02/11/2026):** the Drug history modal and the Records page show each medicine as its generic (`Tablet. Paracetamol 500 mg`, the form and strength as written) where the medicine chart gives exactly one; otherwise as written, with a quiet note. It is display only — the record keeps the brand line as written, Move to Rx puts that brand line on the ℞ pad (a doctor prescribes a brand), and the printed prescription is unchanged (open question for the physician). See `client/CLAUDE.md`, Drug history.

- **Current medications = the PREVIOUS visit (physician's sheet, 18/10/2026, item 2, and "only the previous visit" in chat the same day):** Drug history's Current tab is every entry of the latest dated visit before today, plus what the doctor adds to Current by hand today; today's ℞ is in NEITHER tab (it is on the pad, and the mirror still records it, so it is "the previous visit" next time); everything older — and the no-date marker `01/01/2000` — is Distant past. Visit 1: hand-added Current stays Current. Visit 2: visit 1 is Current. Visit 3: visit 2 is Current, visit 1 has moved to Distant past. Until this, Current was "dated today" (today's ℞), and the previous visit's medicines had already gone to Distant past on the day they were needed. Derived, never stored (`client/src/lib/drugHistorySplit.ts`). The prescribing alerts' "current drug history" (90-day window, `rxAlerts.ts`) is a separate rule and was not changed.
- **Drug history is OFF the printed sheet by default (same sheet, item 1):** every new visit starts with ⊘ Hidden on; pressing it prints the section for that visit. Entries untouched.

- **℞ → Drug history mirror (2026-09-07):** every medicine on today's ℞ pad is stamped with `ptDate` and merged into `Patient.drugHistory` **live, as it is typed** (physician's decision), so it shows in **Current medications** during the visit and as Distant past on the next one. Until this, the ℞ pad and Drug history were two lists that never spoke: "Current medications" held only what the doctor re-typed into the modal by hand. The mirror **withdraws only entries it added itself** (a hand-typed medication is untouchable), **completes nothing** (no invented dose), **persists through the writers that already existed** rather than a new one, and is **stripped back out of the printed sheet** — today's medicines are the ℞ table, and repeating them under "Drug history" would say the patient was already on them before this visit. `client/src/lib/rxDrugHistory.ts`, pinned in `rxDrugHistory.test.ts`; see `client/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Add medicine, Drug history (2026-10-14):** a "+ Add medicine" row on each Drug-history tab — Current medications and Distant past — writes one brand-new, date-stamped line to `Patient.drugHistory` (name matched against the real medicines catalogue, the same lookup the ℞ pad's own box uses; dose/food/duration free text). It is strictly an ADD: neither tab becomes editable, and an existing stored row still cannot be changed or removed from this modal — the mirror above withdraws only what it itself added, and a typed addition was never in that set. A Current addition is stamped with the visit date; a Distant-past one carries no date and groups under "Earlier (no date)". An exact duplicate (same medicine *and* same dose/food/duration) is refused; the same medicine at a different dose is always a new line. It reaches today's ℞ only through the existing Move to Rx. Before Save & print the line is part of the visit (parked with it, written by the print); after it, it is appended to the patient's record at once by the server (`POST /patients/:id/drug-history`) — never a whole list from a device — and refused, with the reason, for a supervising doctor, who cannot write the owner's record (19/10/2026). `client/src/lib/drugHistoryAdd.ts`; see `client/CLAUDE.md`.
- **Drug history table, Edit and Add in Patient's Prescriptions & reports (physician's sheet, 07/11/2026):** the section is a table of courses — Drug name, Dose, Duration, Start date, End date, Total number — with the tapering lines under their medicine. **End = Start + the days of every line, one after the other** (a 7-day course from 06/11 ends 13/11), **Total = units a day × days summed over the lines** (1+0+1 for 7 days is 14); a duration or dose the app cannot read leaves them blank, never a guess. **Current medication is what is still going on — End today or later, or Continue** — and comes first; the rest by **generic (A–Z)** or by **start date**, the physician's two sorts. The name is the generic with the brand as stored in brackets (`Paracetamol (Tablet. Napa 500 mg)`). **Edit** puts a start date or a duration right in the patient's record — the first time a stored drug-history line may be changed — and **Add** records a medicine with its start date; both go to the record at once, and a change is also what **Health monitoring** draws (the bar runs from Start to End). The same medicine twice with different dates and durations is two rows. What Edit may change is the date and the duration, never the name, dose or food; a line on today's ℞ is changed on the pad. Details: `client/CLAUDE.md` (Drug history), `server/CLAUDE.md` (Rule 2), `docs/API.md`.
