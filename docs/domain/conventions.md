# Domain — Conventions and communication

> Part of the shared vocabulary ([glossary](../DOMAIN.md)). Each entry is a concept with the physician's decisions behind it, moved here word for word from `docs/DOMAIN.md` on 2026-11-15. Keep it current in the same commit as the change (PRIME DIRECTIVE rule 7), and its line in the glossary with it.

## Conventions

- **Add is a circled plus (02/11/2026):** every "add" in the doctor app is drawn as a plus in a ring (`plusCircle`); a `+` that means zoom, open a group or a clinical grade (Anaemia `++`) is not an "add" and stays as it was.

- **Night mode (2026-10-15):** a ☀ / ☾ switch beside BAN / EN turns the doctor app dark grey (not black, the physician's choice), easy on the eyes at night. It is remembered per user per device and not mirrored. Light mode is unchanged. Printouts, patient images and rich text (the personal note, the printed header / footer) stay on white paper in both modes. The admin console has no night mode. See `client/CLAUDE.md`.
- **BAN / EN typing (2026-09-23):** a header switch that turns phonetic (Avro-style) Bangla typing on for the fields the physician named — Chief complaints, Previous complaints, Note, Plan, Advice and the OPD ℞ pad's dose/food/duration — and nowhere else; any other field keeps English and says so. **The keyboard never converts a number** (a dose is typed `1+0+1`, a half `0.5`). **Since 2026-10-14 BAN also WRITES the OPD ℞ pad's dose / food / duration in Bangla, and EN writes them back** (physician's decision): the text itself changes, and is saved and printed so — the physician's own table, word for word (খাবারের আগে, চলবে, ৭ দিন…), and the digits of those cells (`১+০+১`, `০.৫` — a decimal point never becomes the dari). Text outside the table stays as written. It runs on a press of the switch, on a cell committed in BAN, and on a line inserted from "Your usual", a template, Add Bulk Medicine or Move to Rx — never on a pad arriving from another device. "Already on ℞" sees one instruction in either language. **Since 2026-10-15 the three columns are also SHOWN in the current language** wherever they are listed — "Your usual", Add Bulk Medicine and Drug history (display only; what is stored is untouched) — and "Your usual" learns BAN and EN as one habit (`server/src/rx-habits/rxLanguage.ts`). See `client/CLAUDE.md`.

- **What a colour means (2026-10-05, physician chose the blue brand):** the app speaks in five hues and each has one job. **Blue (`C.pri`)** is the brand and anything that can be pressed or is selected. **Green (`C.ok`)** means done / normal / saved / stable — a normal BMI, an OPD visit marked Complete, an IPD patient marked Stable, the medication bars on the health-trend chart. **Amber** means needs attention, and is also the ⊘ "hidden from print" mark and the Keep-eye toggle. **Red** means critical, contraindicated or destructive. **Light blue (`C.info`)** is passive information (Discharge, bed numbers). Until that date the brand was green and doubled as "fine": a Save button and a normal result were the same colour. Never put the brand colour on a status. The **printed prescription** carries the same blue on five accents (the rule under the letterhead, ℞, section titles, the follow-up interval, the MHS badge) — the physician asked for the paper to match on 2026-10-05 — but written out in `prescriptionDoc.ts`, which imports no theme so that editing the app can never move the document.
- **A unit is shown as it is written (2026-10-05).** Lab, calculator and examination labels were forced to capitals by a style rule, which changed what they said: `g/dL` read `G/DL`, `fL` read `FL`, and a calculator's `µL` read `ΜL`. They now appear exactly as the investigation catalog and the calculator definitions write them. No label was reworded. **Awaiting the physician's word on one point:** test NAMES also lost their capitals with this (`RBC (million/uL)`, `Hb (g/dL)`, `Neutrophil count (cells/uL)`); if capitals are wanted back on names, they go back on the name only, never on the unit.

- **Asking before something is taken away (2026-10-06).** Removing an assistant, a ward, a ward's member, a supervising doctor, a relative, a patient's photo, an order-sheet page or a template asks first, in the app's own box: the question names the thing, the button is the verb ("Delete ward"), and Cancel is the one already selected, so a stray Enter keeps what was there. A failure the doctor has to know about ("2 images were NOT added: a different patient was opened", "Prescription saved, but the printable sheet could not be built") is said the same way and stays until it is acknowledged. So is a save that did not happen — "The prescription was not saved." / "The visit was not saved for later.", with what they can check ("Check your internet connection and try again.") and "Everything you wrote is still here." — and a history that was not updated after a saved prescription (18/10/2026). Until then these were a line under the Save buttons, drawn in the "saved" green, gone after three to five seconds, under the print sheet after Save & print, and two of them asked "Is the API running?". The line now says only what was saved (green) and an empty form (amber). The IPD sheet's two Saves say it the same way since 20/10/2026 ("The changes to this admission were not saved."), and a follow-up's vitals stay in their boxes until the server has them — they went into the log at the press, and a save that failed left them there, looking recorded. These used to be the browser's own boxes, which a user can switch off for the page — after which the app removed nothing and said nothing. Because the screen stays live while the question is open, the answer applies only to the patient who was on screen when it was asked.
- **A typed date and a recorded moment are two things, shown two ways (2026-10-09).** A date the doctor types — the visit date, a date of birth, the date of a finding — is `dd/mm/yyyy`, as it always was. A moment the SYSTEM recorded — admitted, an order-sheet page added, a follow-up entry, a note last saved — is written day first with the month as a word and a 24-hour time, `9 Oct 2026 · 17:04` (`client/src/lib/stamp.ts`), the same on every machine whatever language its browser is set to. Before this the browser wrote them: one machine showed `Oct 9`, `Oct 9, 2026` and `9 Oct 2026 · 17:04` on two ward screens, and `Last saved 10/9/2026` reads as the 10th of September beside this app's own dates. A month written as a word cannot be read as a day.
- **A page opened to be read is shown whole (image viewer, 2026-10-09).** A photographed prescription, a report or a ward order sheet opens entirely on the screen, at fit, with no control drawn over it — the arrows and the zoom bar have room of their own, and on a phone the arrows sit beside the bar under the page. This is the same rule as the big, uncropped order-sheet tiles: a doctor makes out a dose from this picture, and the line that is cut off or covered is the one that carried it. Magnifying is always the doctor's own act, and a dragged page moves exactly with the hand.

- **Whatever covers the screen keeps the keyboard (2026-10-06, completed 2026-10-12).** A popup — a field's list, the Investigation popup, the image viewer, the print sheet — takes the focus when it opens, keeps Tab on its own controls, closes on Escape, and gives the focus back to what opened it. **One exception, for the record's sake:** the print sheet opened by "Save & print" does not give the focus back to that button. It saves; with the focus on it, Enter or Space would file the same consultation a second time. (The "Your workstations" drawer and the wall a secondary account sees are not yet popups of this kind.) The reason is a safety one, not tidiness: with the print sheet covering the whole screen, the focus used to stay on the page underneath, and three presses of Tab reached the patient chat's message box, where Enter sends. A question raised over a popup ("Remove this page?") owns Escape while it is up, so one key press never closes two things.

- **A caption and its box (2026-10-12).** Pressing the words above a box ("Bed", "Mobile", "Hb") puts the cursor in that box, on every form. A caption never does more than that — it does not tick, choose or type — and a caption over a value that is only shown (BMI, ideal body weight), or over a box that is locked, does nothing. Only what the code marks as a caption behaves this way; "Mirror devices" in the header is a switch, not a caption.
- **What the screen says does not depend on the machine (2026-10-12).** A clinical number, a date and a test's name are written by the app, never by the browser's language setting or by a style rule. A calculator's `1,500` is `1,500` on every laptop (a German-language browser used to show `1.500`); a recorded moment is `9 Oct 2026 · 17:04` everywhere; and a test's name keeps the case the catalog gives it, because `eGFR` in capitals is another test. Two doctors looking at the same patient on two machines must be reading the same thing. It is about the record as well as the screen: a calculator's sentence is what is filed, and printed, when its result is added. (One kind of browser did see a change: Indian English grouped 100,000 and over as `1,50,000`, and now reads `150,000` like the rest.)

## Communication

- **Revision Notes (physician's sheet, 18/10/2026, item 4):** a tab at the end of the tab row (after BAN / EN and ☀ / ☾; on a phone, a card on "More") holding the SIGNED-IN user's own notebook — written like a Word page (bold, highlight), drawn as A4 pages that are added as the writing runs on, printed (Print prints the notebook alone), and searched with Ctrl+F (the app's own search box: matches counted and stepped through with Enter). Keyed by the authenticated user (`DoctorRevisionNote`, `manual-revision-notes.sql`): nobody else reads it, an assistant included. It saves by itself two seconds after typing stops and when the tab is left; a save from a copy another screen has since replaced is refused (409) and the typing stays on the screen. Every save and every read names the user whose notebook it is, and the server refuses either one for anyone but the signed-in user (409): two tabs of one browser share one login, and a tab still showing one doctor saved under the next one's session — one doctor's text became the other's notebook — and, coming back to the tab, was shown the other's notebook (release reviews, 18/10/2026). No patient data; not logged.
- **My Personal Note for This Patient (2026-09-24):** a section at the bottom of the OPD sidebar where the SIGNED-IN user keeps a private, formatted note about a patient (bold, highlight, font size, colour). **Strictly private:** stored by the authenticated user's id (`DoctorPatientNote`, `manual-doctor-patient-note.sql`), never the workstation doctor's, so an assistant, the owner and a supervising doctor each see only their own; never logged to the activity feed, never mirrored, never printed with the prescription. One evolving note per (user, patient) — a new visit does not reset it. Name/age/sex/address/mobile are auto-filled from the patient's details and **frozen at the first save** (physician's decision). Edit / Save / Print; Print prints the note alone. **🔒 Sensitive information (2026-09-25):** a toolbar toggle beside Highlight — text typed while it is on (or a selection it is pressed on) is marked violet with a dashed underline and a lock (stored as an inert `<a href="mhs-sensitive:1">`, see `client/CLAUDE.md` for why a link), distinct from any highlighter; when the note carries such text, Print asks "with" or "without sensitive information", and "without" leaves it off the page entirely (nothing stands in its place). Images are deliberately not allowed in it (an upload would be reachable by URL). **The note is opened FOR a patient and stays that patient's until it is closed (2026-10-12):** it used to follow the editor, so when another device loaded the next patient under an open note, words typed for one patient were saved into the next one's note, under the next one's details. An open note no longer follows the editor; it is closed and opened again for whoever is loaded. What is typed while a save is on its way is kept as unsaved, and Print prints the text on the screen. See `client/CLAUDE.md`, `server/CLAUDE.md`.
