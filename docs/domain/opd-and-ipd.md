# Domain — OPD and IPD

> Part of the shared vocabulary ([glossary](../DOMAIN.md)). Each entry is a concept with the physician's decisions behind it, moved here word for word from `docs/DOMAIN.md` on 2026-11-15. Keep it current in the same commit as the change (PRIME DIRECTIVE rule 7), and its line in the glossary with it.

## OPD

- **Statistics — Settings → Statistics (physician's sheet, 11/11/2026):** a card right after Payment, and inside it a horizontal menu of two. **Patient statistics:** a bar chart for the practice's OPD queue — by DAYS (every day of a month: a light bar for the day's **total serial**, a dark one for the patients **seen**) or by MONTHS (a bar for the patients seen in each month of a year, the number written above it) — for "All chamber total" or one chamber, with month / year arrows; and five **overall numbers**: Total visit, Average monthly visits, Average daily visits, This month's visit (up to today), Previous month visit. **Seen = what the OPD queue calls Completed** (Done pressed, or the prescription printed — the Completed card's own rule). **Total serial = every queue row of the day**, and a visit belongs to its queue day, never the day it was written. **The averages are calendar averages** (physician's choice, 11/11/2026): the seen visits divided by every day from the first seen visit to today, closed days included, and by every month from that visit's month to this one, this one included — so a clinic that sits three days a week reads lower than its sitting days would show. **Only patients put on the OPD queue are counted**: a prescription written for a patient who was never queued is not a visit here. **Disease statistics:** every Final diagnosis written on the practice's prescriptions with the number of DIFFERENT patients who have it — a patient seen five times with DM is one (physician: "joto patient"). Two writings are one only when they differ in capitals and spacing (`DM` / `dm`); `DM` and `Diabetes mellitus` are two lines, because which names mean one disease is the physician's to say. A prescription holds no chamber, so this is the whole practice's. Read only, no migration; the practice's assistants may open it (physician's decision). See `client/CLAUDE.md`, `server/CLAUDE.md`, `docs/API.md` §5.12d, `FUNCTIONAL-AUDIT.md`.

- **OPD queue by day (physician's sheet, 28/10/2026):** the OPD tab's date is a button that opens a calendar; the queue is booked **per Dhaka calendar day** (`OpdVisit.queueDate`, `yyyy-mm-dd`). Serials (1, 2, 3 …, `position`) can be added and put in order (▲ ▼) for **today or a later day**; a day that has **passed is locked** with what it holds — viewable, never changed, and the server refuses it, not only the screen. Prescribe and Done are for today's patient only. The token (`T-01`) is per queue day and stays with the patient when the order changes. **Each day has its own chamber schedule** (`OpdDaySetting`: start time, an optional break, minutes per patient; a new day starts from the latest earlier day's, and the first patient added to a day saves what it took, so times already told to patients do not move). The time of each serial is **worked out, never stored** (`client/src/lib/opdSchedule.ts`): the first patient at the start time, each next one a step later; whoever would begin inside the break goes to its end; a patient who begins before the break may run into it ("break cut, the slot is let to be long") and the next one waits for its end. The physician's own example is pinned: start 6 PM, break 8–9 PM, 10 minutes — 1st 6:00 PM · 2nd 6:10 PM · … · 12th 7:50 PM · 13th 9:00 PM. The times shown on the list are the **saved** schedule's, never a half-typed box. **Print Queue (30/10/2026):** a button beside the date prints the day on show as a sheet (`client/src/lib/opdQueueDoc.ts`): a header with the practice doctor's name (the workstation's doctor), the day and the patient count, then Serial, Name, Gender, Age (`34y`), Mobile and Appointment time (the saved schedule's) for each patient - no token, marks or status; a day that has passed can be printed. **"Send SMS to all patients" (built 01/11/2026):** see "SMS to a day's queue" below. Pre-set values when nothing was ever saved: 18:00, no break, 10 minutes — the sheet's own example, the physician's to change. `manual-opd-queue-date.sql` must be applied **before** the release is deployed.
  - **Presence, urgency and status (physician's sheet, 29/10/2026).** Each queue row shows the patient's photo (or the app's placeholder) and three boxes: **Present** (records the time, shown under the box, e.g. `6.00pm`), **Critically ill**, **Pt needs urgent attention**. The row is coloured by the strongest mark: Urgent over Critically ill over Present. **Status** is the latest thing true of the visit: neither present nor anything on the pad → empty; marked Present → Waiting; a chief complaint "or any data entry" → History taken; a medicine or investigation written, not yet printed → Prescription pending; printed or Done → Completed (the latest wins). The cards above the list count by these. Since the sheet of 31/10/2026 ("aro deep color … chokhe pore na") the row is drawn in the palette's 100 shade with a bar of the 400 shade down its left edge (was the pale 50 shade alone), in both modes. The list of editor fields that make "History taken" is one constant (`HISTORY_FIELDS`, `server/src/opd/pad-stage.ts`) and is the physician's to confirm. `manual-opd-presence.sql` must be applied **before** the release. See `client/CLAUDE.md`, `server/CLAUDE.md`, `docs/API.md` §5.12.
- **SMS to a day's queue, the SMS wallet, Settings → Payment (physician's sheet, 28/10/2026; built 01/11/2026).** "Send SMS to all patients" beside Print Queue sends every patient of the day on show (today or later — a day that has passed and an empty day are refused by the server) an appointment SMS, after the doctor chooses Bangla or English and has seen every message and its cost. **The message is built on the server** from the physician's two samples (`server/src/sms/appointment-message.ts`): the patient's name as queued, the practice doctor's profile name, the chamber whose queue it is (the one chosen on the screen — its own name and address), and the serial's time — the Print Queue's, from that chamber's saved schedule (`6.10PM` in English; in Bangla wholly Bangla, `সন্ধ্যা ৬:১০`, with a part-of-day word whose ranges beyond সন্ধ্যা are conventional and still the physician's to confirm). **Title (physician, 01/11/2026):** জনাব for a male patient, জনাবা for a female one, from the RECORDED sex; Mr. / Ms. in English by the same rule; none when the sex is not recorded or is Other — never a guess. A number that is missing or not a Bangladeshi mobile is skipped and said. **Counting:** English (GSM-7) 160 characters a message, 153 a part; Bangla (UCS-2) 70, 67 a part — so the same message in Bangla costs more; **1 taka per message counted**. **The wallet is the PRACTICE's** (the workstation doctor's): whole taka, never negative, every change a ledger line (`send`, `refund`, `topup`, `adjust`); a send reserves its cost and returns what did not go, so only messages the gateway took are charged; an unconfigured gateway is refused before anything is taken. The same message to the same number within 30 minutes needs "Send again". **Settings → Payment** (right after Profile): the balance, **Renew** (the doctor's monthly subscription, physician 01/11/2026) and **Buy SMS** — both are requests to the admin (no payment gateway exists, no prices are set by the app), who credits the wallet in the admin console after the payment has come in. Assistants can send from the doctor's wallet; only the doctor sees the history and asks for payment. Open questions in `FUNCTIONAL-AUDIT.md`.
- **Chambers — "My Chamber" (physician's sheet 28/10/2026, item 8):** a doctor who sits at Labaid and at Ibn Sina adds each in **Settings → Chambers** (name and address; the address is what an SMS will use later). Every practice has a **main chamber** — made the first time chambers are read, from the profile's first chamber address, holding every OPD visit from before chambers, and never removable. **"My Chamber"** beside the Prescription tab chooses the chamber the user works in (per user, per practice, per device). **Each chamber has its own OPD list, tokens (each starts at `T-01`), day schedule, and OPD prescription print settings** (Prescription settings → "Settings for"; a chamber with none of its own prints with the doctor's general settings). Chamber ids are checked by the server: another practice's or a removed chamber is refused, never quietly taken for the main one. IPD is untouched. NOT the profile's "Chambers" list (that one is still the profile's).

## IPD

- **Analogue order sheet (correction 3.docx, 2026-08-26):** the ward's own PAPER order sheet, photographed into the admission. Stored as `IpdClinical.analogueSheets` and written ONLY through the per-page `/ipd/:id/analogue` routes, never the admission's Save — a doctor called away mid-round must not lose the pages they just took. Three rules carry the design: the pages are **saved the moment they upload**, removal is **soft** (`removedAt`/`removedBy`, nothing leaves disk, every operation files an `IpdEvent`), and the list **cannot be reordered** because its order is the chronology of the round. The IPD detail now offers three views the doctor switches between — `F/U & Digital Order`, `Analogue & Digital Order`, `F/U & Analogue order` — with no default: an unchosen state opens the page exactly as it always has. The same document renamed IPD's ℞ heading from "Order sheet" to **Prescription**, reversing the 2026-07-30 decision; "order sheet" on the ward now means the paper one.

- **IPD admits by mobile number first (same sheet, item 5):** as the OPD queue since 15/10/2026 — a patient saved on the number is chosen and their name shown, "Add New" creates one; the name is never typed on the admit form, so every admission is tied to a patient record (the server refuses a record the practice cannot reach).
